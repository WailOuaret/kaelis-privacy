Delete your Kaelis account

Last updated: 26 October 2026

You can request deletion of your Kaelis account and associated personal data at any time.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Kaelis app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings (or Account).
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Confirm with your password when asked.

After you confirm, deletion is scheduled. You may cancel within the grace period shown in the app if that option is available.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email privacy@kaelis.app from the same address used for your Kaelis account, with subject Account deletion request.

Include:

  1. Your account email
  2. A clear request to delete the account

What we delete

We delete or anonymize account profile data, closet/outfit content linked to your account, and related personal records, except limited information we must keep for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or billing obligations.

Timing

We process deletion requests within a reasonable period (typically within 30 days after confirmation / grace period ends).
