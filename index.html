Kaelis Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Kaelis (“we”, “our”, “the App”) is a style and closet companion that helps you organize outfits, explore inspiration, and use AI styling features. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

  • Account data: email, name, password (stored hashed), and profile details you provide.
  • Content you upload: photos of clothing, outfits, avatars, and related media.
  • App activity: closet items, collections, calendar looks, likes, follows/inspires, and AI generation requests.
  • Device / technical data: IP address, app version, and basic diagnostics needed to keep the service secure and reliable.

2. How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and authentication sessions.
  • To provide core features: closet, outfits, feed, search, calendar, notifications, billing, and AI Helper.
  • To process AI image generations you request and manage credit balances.
  • To moderate reports, prevent abuse, and improve reliability and safety.
  • To communicate important service notices (security, account, or billing).

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with service providers that help us run Kaelis (for example hosting, storage, authentication, payments, or AI processing) only as needed to operate the App, and under appropriate safeguards. We may disclose information if required by law.

4. Public content

Content you set as public (for example public outfits or profile details) can be visible to other users. Private items remain restricted to your account according to the App’s privacy settings.

5. Retention & deletion

We keep account and content data while your account is active. You may request account deletion from within the App (Account settings) or by emailing us. After a deletion request, we remove or anonymize personal data within a reasonable period, except where we must retain limited records for legal, security, or billing reasons.

6. Children’s privacy

Kaelis is intended for users 13+. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures such as encrypted transport (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Your choices

  • Update profile and privacy settings in the App.
  • Export or delete your account from Account settings where available.
  • Contact us to ask questions about your data.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@kaelis.app
Support: support@kaelis.app

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of Kaelis after an update means you accept the revised policy.
